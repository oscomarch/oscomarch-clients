What I do

I write and send the emails your subscribers get after they sign up, so they stay longer and each one brings you more money.

I handle everything, from who gets which email to the words and the weekly tests. You have nothing to manage.

Subscribers usually leave at five moments. Here is what I send at each one.

  • 01

    After the first order

    I show them how to use the product and what to expect, so they don't give up in the first few weeks.

  • 02

    Before each charge

    I give them a reason to keep the next delivery, a few days before their card is charged.

  • 03

    When they want a break

    I show them they can skip or change their next delivery in one click, before they go looking for the cancel button.

  • 04

    When they're happy

    I offer the right people a 6 or 12 month plan paid upfront, at the moment they're most ready to say yes.

  • 05

    When they cancel

    I answer the real reason they want to leave, and I leave the door open for them to come back.

How it works

We start with a free audit. Then I take care of everything, and every month you see what it brought in.

  1. 01

    A free audit

    You tell me about your brand in two minutes. Within 48 hours, I send you a video that shows what your subscribers receive today and what I would change first.

  2. 02

    We measure where you start

    We pick the number that matters most, usually how many months a subscriber stays. A small random group keeps your current emails, so we see exactly what my emails add, without counting your ads or your promotions.

  3. 03

    I write, send and test new emails every week. Each month, you get the result and the plan for the next month.

DTC brands that sell on subscription, like supplements, coffee, pet food or skincare. You have around 1,000 active subscribers or more, on Shopify with Klaviyo and Recharge, Skio or Loop. I work with three brands at a time.

What worked

Two subject lines did most of the work, “Lock in your price before it goes back up.” and “€37.80 sitting idle on your subscription.” Of the people who opened them, 21.3% and 17.8% clicked, against 3.99% on average for the campaign.

Both do the same thing. They put an exact number on what the reader is about to lose. Nobody wants to leave money on the table.

What didn't

One email was opened by almost half the people who got it (49.7%), but barely anyone clicked (1.9%). The subject line promised more than the email gave. A big open rate means nothing on its own, and I'd rather tell you than hide it.

What I won't pretend

This is a comparison with the month before, not a controlled test. Some of these people would have subscribed anyway. And most email tools are generous when they count which sales came from an email.

That's exactly why, with you, a small group of customers doesn't get the new emails from day one. So the number we look at is the real one.

Abby hired me for three campaigns in a row, for Black Friday, January and the summer, with two different heads of marketing.

Questions

Will it still sound like us?

Yes. I read everything you've written before, and I talk to your customers. If it doesn't sound like you, it doesn't go out.

What if it doesn't work?

You'll see it in the numbers, because we measure from day one. Then you only pay the fixed fee, and you can stop after the first months.

How is my share calculated?

It's based on what the emails bring in above the starting point we set together, compared with the group that keeps your current emails. So you only pay for what they really add.

When will I see results?

The first sends go out in the first month. The real progress shows after two or three months, once the system has learned enough about your customers.
