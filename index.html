You have a shop, a brand or a roastery, with a few thousand people who follow you by email. People who already bought from you, or who are still making up their mind.

You write to them when you find the time, a bit by feel, or you send basic campaigns. You're not quite sure what works, or why.

Then you already have the hardest part. People who know you, and who have often trusted you already. What's missing is the right way to talk to them.

How it works

For you, it's simple.

From day one, I dive into everything you have. Your past emails, your numbers, your customer reviews, their questions to support. I call a few of them. I want to understand who they are, what they love, and what makes them buy. I want to be as close to them as possible, so they feel it in every email they get.

From there, I handle everything. The writing, the segments, the flows, the optimization, and the analysis of every result.

And I stay reachable all the time. An idea, a last-minute drop, a doubt, you write to me and I take care of it. We decide the direction together, but I carry the work. You keep control, without the load.

Every month, you get the same thing. One clear number, the one you judge me on. A page that sums up what we learned about your customers. The plan for the next month. And a short call to talk it through.

Results

What happened with one of the clients I work with

Over €500,000 in sales,
across 3 campaigns.

At first, Abby talked to its customers in five groups. Campaign after campaign, we saw it wasn't enough. Within the same group, people reacted in completely different ways.

So we went finer, up to more than fifteen segments, with a message written for each one. We tested, adjusted, started again, fast. Until we got it right for every group.

In total, over half a million euros in sales. And a much sharper understanding of the people Abby talks to.

I've been writing for brands like Abby since I was 16, between France and the US. I've also worked on growth for several startups, including Fastflo, Nao Labs and Astera.

Questions

Why not just use AI?

You can. Your competitors already do, and their emails all look the same. I use AI to go faster on the analysis. Never to write to your customers.

When will I see results?

The first sends go out in the first month. The real progress shows after two or three months, once the system has learned enough about your customers.

Which tool do you work with?

The one you already have. Klaviyo, Brevo, Shopify Email or another, I adapt. No need to change anything on your side.

Are you an agency?

No. You work with me, directly, from day one to the last send.
