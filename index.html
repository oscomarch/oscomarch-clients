The problem

You know your emails could do more.
You just never have the time.

Your flows were set up once, a while ago. Nobody has really looked at them since.

Every new customer costs more in ads than the last. And most of them order once, then never again. Subscribers pause, then cancel, and you don't really know why.

When you do send something, it's often a discount. Because that's the only thing you're sure works.

But you already have the hardest part. People who know you, and who have often trusted you already. What's missing is the right way to talk to them.

What I run

I take it off your plate.
All of it.

  • Segmentation

    Who your customers really are, and what each of them needs to hear.

  • Lifecycle flows

    After the first order. Before the bag runs out. When someone drifts away. Before a subscriber cancels.

  • Campaigns

    Drops, launches, seasons. Without a discount code every time.

  • Testing

    Every send is also a test. I look at who clicks, who buys, who comes back, and I take a lesson from it for the next ones.

I work with the audience you already have. To find a new one, I'll introduce you to the right people.

A fixed monthly fee to run the system. Plus a share of what it brings in above the baseline we set together.

If it doesn't work, I earn less. That's the point.

Questions

Will it still sound like us?

Yes. I read everything you've written before, and I talk to your customers. If it doesn't sound like you, it doesn't go out.

What if it doesn't work?

You'll see it in the numbers, because we measure from day one. Then you only pay the fixed fee, and you can stop after the first months.

How is my share calculated?

On what the system brings in above the baseline we set at the start, compared with the group that doesn't get the new emails. So you only pay for what it really adds.

What do you need from us?

Access to your store, your email tool and your past numbers. An hour of your time at the start. Then a short call each month.

When will I see results?

The first sends go out in the first month. The real progress shows after two or three months, once the system has learned enough about your customers.
