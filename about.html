About me

I run retention for DTC brands that sell on subscription. I make subscribers stay longer, skip fewer deliveries, and move to longer plans.

At work

I build the system, I write every email, and I look at the numbers every week.

I work with three brands at a time, and part of my pay depends on what changes.

Outside work

I grew up near Tours. My dad is a novelist and a painter. I started writing at 15, and at 16 I moved to Paris on my own. Writing emails for brands is what paid for it.

Since then, I've studied at La Sorbonne, at Columbia in New York, and now at ESCP. I teach microeconomics and statistics at La Sorbonne.

I skate a lot. Specialty coffee has become an obsession, and my days start with an espresso. And I play guitar.
